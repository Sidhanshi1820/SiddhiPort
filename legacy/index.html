// about

Breaking things to understand how to defend them.

I'm a Computer Science student focused on cybersecurity — the intersection of network behavior, adversarial thinking, and the tooling needed to make sense of both. Most of my work sits at the boundary between raw packet data and the models that try to explain it: writing analyzers in Go, prototyping detection logic in Python, and testing how far machine learning can go in strengthening — or breaking — cryptographic systems.

Outside of security-specific work, I build interactive web tools when a problem is better explained with an interface than a report.
